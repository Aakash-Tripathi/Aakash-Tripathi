## 🛠️ Research & Software

- **🐝 [HONeYBEE](https://github.com/lab-rasool/HoneyBee)** — Framework that converts text, pathology slides, radiology, and omics into patient embeddings for classification, retrieval, clustering, and survival analysis; evaluated on 11,428 TCGA patients across 33 cancer types. [Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Lab-Rasool/TCGA) · [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41746-025-02003-4)
- **🧠 [MINDS](https://github.com/lab-rasool/MINDS)** — Multimodal Integration of Oncology Data System for patient-centered aggregation, harmonization, and linkage of data from cancer repositories. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.3390/s24051634)
- **📄 CLEVER** — LLM-agent system that extracts variables, patient timelines, and ICD codes from medical records while retaining document evidence for review; deployed at institution scale at Moffitt. [Pathology extraction paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.labinv.2025.104272)
- **📈 SeNMo, RMSurv, EAGLE, PARADIGM** — Survival modeling and omics representations using fusion, attention, and graph learning. [SeNMo](https://doi.org/10.3390/ijms26157358) · [RMSurv](https://doi.org/10.1177/11769351251376192) · [EAGLE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.22446)
- **🧬 [HetMoE & ShiftSmooth](https://github.com/lab-rasool/TFBS)** — Transcription factor binding site prediction with a heterogeneous mixture of experts, plus motif attribution. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.3390/math14142489)
- **🩺 TheBlueScrubs & CHECK** — A 25B+ token curated medical corpus and a framework for detecting and reducing LLM hallucinations. [Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/TheBlueScrubs/TheBlueScrubs-v1)
